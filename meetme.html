what i shoot with

Not much of it, and none of it is why a photo works. But people ask.

  1. 01

    Sigma 24-70mm f/2.8 DG DN Art

    The walk-around lens. Food, cars up close, anything that happens within arm's reach.

  2. 02

    Sony FE 70-200mm f/2.8 GM OSS

    Concerts and pit lanes. Light for what it is, and on the camera more than anything else.

  3. 03

    Sony FE 200-600mm f/5.6-6.3 G OSS

    Aircraft. Most of the aviation album is this lens, a lot of heat and a lot of waiting.

  4. 04

    Sony α1

    The body. Fifty megapixels at thirty frames a second, and it hasn't lost a jet yet.
